About Our Team

Hadiya Sarwari

Hadiya Sarwari is the co-founder and master baker of H&M Bakery. She completed advanced bakery and artisan bread courses in Italy and has more than 5 years of experience in creating fresh bread, croissants, and pastries. Her passion for baking and commitment to quality make every product fresh and delicious.

Mariyam Baltabaeva

Mariyam Baltabaeva is the co-founder and head pastry chef of H&M Bakery. She completed professional pastry training in Paris and specializes in cakes, desserts, and modern bakery decoration. Her creative recipes and attention to detail have helped H&M Bakery become one of the most popular bakery brands among dessert lovers.

H&M Bakery

H&M Bakery was founded by two passionate pastry professionals who share a love for baking and creating unforgettable desserts. Our mission is to bring happiness to every customer through fresh, high-quality products and excellent service.
